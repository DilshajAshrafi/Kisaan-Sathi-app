Soil & Pest Care

Healthy soil and early pest detection support stronger crops. Regular observation and informed decisions can help reduce crop damage.

Helpful steps

  • Use soil testing to understand pH and nutrient levels before choosing inputs.
  • Inspect plants regularly for unusual spots, wilting, holes, or visible pests.
  • Identify the problem before applying pesticides, and follow product labels and local guidance.
  • Consider crop rotation, field sanitation, and integrated pest management practices.

Crop symptoms can have several causes. Share clear photos and details about the crop and location with a qualified agriculture expert before treating an unfamiliar issue.

A simple field-ready checklist

A few regular checks can help you spot problems early and make more informed farm decisions.

🪴

Start with the soil

Check soil condition and choose nutrients based on a soil test, not guesswork.

💧

Plan water wisely

Match watering to crop needs and local conditions to avoid waste or stress.

🔎

Check crops regularly

Look for unusual spots, insects, or wilting early; ask an expert before treatment.
