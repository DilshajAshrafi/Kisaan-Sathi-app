Scheme Guidance

Explore private and government support options, and check the eligibility and terms before applying.

How to get started

  1. Choose the kind of support you need for your farm.
  2. Check the scheme's eligibility rules and application dates.
  3. Keep identity, bank, land, and other requested documents ready.
  4. Apply through the official government portal or your local agriculture office.
Private Schemes

Banks, cooperatives, agricultural companies, and nonprofit organizations may offer farm loans, insurance, equipment financing, or other support. Contact the provider directly to confirm current offers, eligibility, fees, and repayment terms.

• Agricultural & Rural Initiatives

  1. ITC e-Choupal: A private sector initiative equipping rural farmers with digital infrastructure to access real-time weather forecasts, localized agricultural knowledge, and transparent market prices, bypassing intermediaries.
  2. Tata Trusts Lakhpati Kisan Program: Focuses on systematically enhancing the annual income of marginalized rural farmers by introducing advanced multi-cropping practices, irrigation technologies, and direct market linkages.
  3. Reliance Foundation Rural Transformation: Provides specialized grants, technical agronomy assistance, and water management infrastructure development to vulnerable farming communities.

• Business Grants & Financial Support

  1. HDFC Bank Parivartan Grants: Awards significant financial grants to social startups, incubators, and NGOs working specifically in agriculture, environmental sustainability, and rural livelihood development.
  2. DBS Foundation Social Enterprise Grant: Awards zero-equity grant funding to businesses that demonstrate a scalable model addressing critical social, environmental, or agricultural issues.
  3. ICICI Bank Startup Ecosystem: Provides emerging enterprises with specialized corporate banking solutions, tailored collateral-free credit lines, and direct networking access to private equity investors.

Scheme availability, eligibility, and application requirements can change. Confirm government scheme details with an official portal or local agriculture office. For private offers, verify all terms directly with the provider before applying.

Government Schemes

Government programs may offer eligible farmers support such as financial assistance, crop insurance, credit, irrigation resources, or help with farm development.

• Agriculture & Farming Support

  1. PM KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi): Provides direct income support of ₹6,000 per year to landholding farmer families across three equal installments.
  2. Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY): A crop insurance scheme protecting farmers against financial losses from crop failure due to unpredictable natural calamities or pests.
  3. PM KUSUM: Subsidizes the installation of standalone solar water pumps and grid-connected renewable power plants for farmers to ensure energy security.
  4. Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF): Provides medium to long-term debt financing facilities (with interest subvention) for post-harvest management infrastructure and community farming assets.

• Business Loans, Grants & Subsidies

  1. Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY): Offers collateral-free loans up to ₹10 lakh to non-corporate, non-farm small and micro-enterprises through commercial banks and NBFCs.
  2. Prime Minister’s Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP): A credit-linked subsidy program aimed at generating employment opportunities in rural and urban areas by funding the establishment of micro-enterprises.
  3. Stand-Up India: Facilitates bank loans between ₹10 lakh and ₹1 crore to at least one SC/ST borrower and one woman borrower per bank branch to set up a greenfield enterprise in manufacturing, services, or trading.
  4. Startup India Seed Fund Scheme: Provides early-stage startups with financial assistance for proof of concept, prototype development, product trials, and commercialization.
